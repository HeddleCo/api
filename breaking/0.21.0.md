# 0.21.0 maturity spine — eight Shipped→Planned demotions + ProvisionAgentRootedAccount prune

This coordinated pre-1.0 cut demotes eight RPCs from the maturity their service
otherwise inherits (`SHIPPED`) back to `SERVICE_MATURITY_PLANNED`. A demotion
withdraws a previously advertised shipped guarantee, so it is reported here as
breaking under the repo's pre-1.0 contract-maturity policy even though no wire
shape changes.

## Removed RPC — `IdentityService/ProvisionAgentRootedAccount` (api#187 PR-B)

The `IdentityService/ProvisionAgentRootedAccount` RPC and its
`ProvisionAgentRootedAccountRequest` / `ProvisionAgentRootedAccountResponse`
messages are removed by owner decision. The deferred-human account path it
described is superseded by the deferred-human owner-key claim ceremony, whose
wire contract is installed additively on existing requests (see the additive
members below): `RegisterPublicKeyRequest.claim_deferred_human = 16` and
`BootstrapOwnerRootRequest.owner_key_binding = 5`. Deleting an advertised RPC
is a hard wire-surface removal and is reported here as breaking. The method and
message type names are retired and must not be reused. The weft-side migration
(delete handler/dispatch/allowlist entry, move the deferred-human install onto
`BootstrapOwnerRoot` + tag 5 / `RegisterPublicKey` + tag 16) is a separate weft
companion PR after 0.21.0 releases.

The eight demoted RPCs (a `maturity: SERVICE_MATURITY_PLANNED` override was
added to each `rpc_contract`):

- `IdentityService/StoreProviderToken`
- `RegistryService/ListActors`
- `RegistryService/ListWorktrees`
- `CollaborationService/ListDiscussionsByStates`
- `RepositoryService/ListStateAttachments`
- `RepositoryService/ListThreadHistories`
- `StateReviewService/RecordCheckAck`
- `WorkflowService/StreamWorkspaceSummary`

## Non-breaking changes in the same batch

The same batch promotes eight RPCs to their inherited `SHIPPED` maturity by
deleting their `maturity: SERVICE_MATURITY_PLANNED` override. Advertising a
stronger guarantee is additive, not breaking:

- Cutover to Shipped: `IdentityService/ListInstallationRepositories`.
- Promote to Shipped: `IdentityService/CreateSignupInvite`,
  `IdentityService/ListSignupInvites`.

`IdentityService/PromoteAgentAccount` deliberately remains `PLANNED`.
`IdentityService/ClaimSignupInvite` and `IdentityService/CreateAgentAccount`
also remain `PLANNED` to preserve their existence-hidden, invite-gated abuse
posture (guarded by `tests/test_signup_contract.py`); they are still admitted
over weft through weft's `admit_weft_deployment` allowlist.
`IdentityService/RegisterGitHubInstallation`,
`OperationService/SetRemoteLink`, and `OperationService/GetRemoteLink` remain
`PLANNED` pending their Weft handlers (guarded by
`tests/transport_contract.rs`) — their cutover/promotion is deferred to a
later release.

The batch also adds three purely additive enum/message members at their next
free numbers:

- `DiscussionKind.DISCUSSION_KIND_IMPORTED = 3`.
- `FeedItemKind.FEED_ITEM_KIND_IMPORTED_DISCUSSION = 6`.
- `WhoAmIResponse.root_status = 16` with a new `RootStatus` enum
  (`ROOT_STATUS_UNSPECIFIED`, `ROOT_STATUS_ROOTED`, `ROOT_STATUS_ROOTLESS`,
  `ROOT_STATUS_UNCLAIMED_AGENT`) per api#131.
- `RegisterPublicKeyRequest.claim_deferred_human = 16`
  (`SignedOwnerKeyTransition`) — the deferred-human claim transition installed
  atomically with a registration (api#187 PR-B).
- `BootstrapOwnerRootRequest.owner_key_binding = 5` (`OwnerKeyBinding`) — the
  self-asserted UUID-to-key binding installed atomically with the owner root
  (api#187 PR-B).

Additive members are not reported as breaking.

## Why no compatibility shim

All pre-1.0 consumers exact-pin the package and move in lockstep. The eight
demotions realign advertised maturity with the handlers that actually ship;
retaining a stale `SHIPPED` guarantee to keep the contract-maturity policy
silent would misrepresent surface no producer serves. The break is documented
here rather than suppressed.
