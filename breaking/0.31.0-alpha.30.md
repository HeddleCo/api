# 0.31.0-alpha.30

Native witness evidence semantics change; no signed layout or purpose domain changes.

A LocalKey `LocalIntegration` dependency resolves as local work through its
original native proof and the same Thread's exact witnessed ownership claim
(purpose 2), including its observed frontier and cross-Thread source closure.
Rust and TypeScript apply this rule to authority and landing dependencies.
Local integrations cannot be purpose-4 hosted executions; LocalKey integrations
cannot receive purpose-2 authority sidecars, including empty-envelope receipts.
Account-authored integrations continue to require exact purpose-2 authority.

Consumers must verify the original native signatures, causal parents, source
revision and result State ancestry with the native codecs before mutation.
LocalKey publishers must equal the target genesis's immutable local owner;
local work and its closure must lie in the selected claim/resolution frontier's
ancestry. Descendants beyond its signed cutoff reject.
No backward-compatible bridge or synthetic receipts. The coordinated cutover
and SyncService mandatory-gate policy are unchanged.

Adds one signed local capture → integration → push control and six signed
portable negatives, plus a signed ancestor-frontier control and three native
owner/cutoff negatives. Two alpha.28 native wire vectors are intentionally
replaced: `distinct_owner_chains` and `missing_selected_owner_chain`. The former
now admits the original owner at 1000 s and the rotated owner at 1100 s after
activation at 1050 s; the old positive selected a future rotation and stale
owner identity. The latter removes only that corrected positive's selected
chain. Every other existing native vector, the import fixture, signed layouts
and domains, and the retirement archive/proofs remain unchanged. All seven
release version files advance together from alpha.29.
