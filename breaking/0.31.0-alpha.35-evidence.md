## PR #358 fix-round evidence (supersedes the original interface)

The policy walker now hashes canonical fields 1–10 on every selected record,
requires a policy body and enforces predecessor-set containment. This follows
`GROW_ONLY_SET_UNION`: each successor must retain every earlier revoked key ID;
subtraction rejects even with a freshly recomputed hash/signature.

Alpha.33/34 import/native callers now reject malformed policy bodies,
commitments, carrier order and duplicate records that the old completeness-only
walker admitted. Their valid existing vectors remain unchanged. The existing native
`historical_policy_requires_authentic_preimage_and_owner_signature` probe
changes rejection stage: its tampered `complete_export` policy body now returns
portable `Canonical` before native policy-preimage verification; owner-signature
refusal remains unchanged. No alpha.33/34
fixture needed regeneration: all 60 pre-existing fixture files remain byte-identical
to the pre-fix head `690c7785`. The generator regenerated only
`tests/fixtures/writer-authority-alpha35.json`: its 23 original vectors are
byte-identical and 36 vectors were added. Fixture SHA-256:
`dce1d8ab4b930c9242f40d54294180dca1403f27541ef175d29fdfbddc36f173`.

All carried policy bodies are checked before a zero-head return, preventing
a record claiming the no-policy sentinel from supplying unchecked revocations.
The new `zero_head_uncommitted_policy` negative and `zero_head_control` preserve
a valid signed no-policy statement and prove the shortcut cannot bypass hashing.

Added vectors:

- Bundle controls: `p2_owner_bundle`, `p4_owner_bundle`,
  `ownership_counterparty_control`.
- P2/P4 negatives: `p2_self_signed_owner_uuid`,
  `p4_self_signed_owner_uuid`, `p2_duplicate_history_poisoning`,
  `p4_duplicate_history_poisoning`, `p2_envelope_not_op_author`,
  `requester_not_token_subject`, `kind_2_counterparty_revoked`,
  `kind_3_counterparty_revoked`, `p4_non_review`.
- Both `native_policy_` and `import_policy_` prefixes: `chain` (control),
  `stripped`, `absent`, `reordered`, `duplicated`, `subtracted`,
  `predecessor_stripped` (negatives).
- Zero-head policies: `zero_head_control`, `zero_head_uncommitted_policy`.
- History facts: `verified_rotate_history`, `verified_recover_history`.
- Exact authenticated attachments: `admitted_original_payload` and
  `admitted_original_statement` (control); `admitted_unknown_payload` and
  `admitted_unknown_statement`; `admitted_bad_signature_payload` and
  `admitted_bad_signature_statement`.

Additional mutations cover stripping an actually revoked publisher from both
native/import policies, duplicate otherwise-valid owner histories, mismatching
request-proof and token-subject keys, unknown historical endpoints, forged
old-owner certificates, unknown protobuf fields on attachments, and mismatched
witness/payload commitments. The counterparty probe uses a later policy only on
its P2 statement; earlier P1 testimony keeps its original unrevoked policy so
its publisher check cannot mask the counterparty guard.
