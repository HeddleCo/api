# 0.31.0-alpha.40: membership floors and per-grant advice

OWNER RULE (2026-10-06): preserve a human owner's access to their own personal
spool, at least one human member, and at least one human ADMINISTRATOR when
members remain. Hosts MUST enforce the floors atomically with every grant
reduction, including self-removal, role/expiry/descendant coverage changes,
ancestor changes, PromoteSpool ancestry loss, DeleteAccount grant cascades on
surviving shared spools, and any invitation/approval grant replacement. weft implements
handlers and transaction/deadline enforcement later; this release is contract,
portable helpers and fixtures only. See the normative
[administration contract](../docs/alpha-v2/administration.md#membership-floors-owner-rule-2026-10-06).

New `ErrorReason` values in the 5xx precondition range are:

- `PERSONAL_SPOOL_OWNER_ACCESS_REQUIRED = 506`;
- `SPOOL_LAST_MEMBER = 507`;
- `SPOOL_LAST_ADMINISTRATOR = 508`.

All use `FAILED_PRECONDITION (9)`. For every reason, `ErrorDetail.field` is
`grant` for revoke/leave/coverage loss, `grant.role` for role replacement, or
`grant.expires_at` for expiry replacement. Precedence is personal owner > last
member > last administrator. Keep resource empty and context absent. Emit these
reasons only after grant-management authorization on the affected spool;
otherwise preserve the existing refusal without revealing hidden membership.

Count distinct live humans at maximum effective role across direct and
applicable descendant-covering ancestor grants. Hidden grants may witness
remaining access but never become advice targets or identify hidden descendant
failures. Agents, pending invitations and support access do not count. Expiry
at exactly server_now is no longer live. Hosts must also guarantee the floors
at future expiry deadlines. Operation authorization is separate from permission
to disclose a floor on an affected spool: safe authorized account cascades or
ancestry changes do not require managing every affected shared/descendant spool;
violating floors there retain the existing opaque refusal. DeleteSpool is separate; personal-spool deletion
remains forbidden under weft#2579.

Regenerate bindings. `Requirement.error = 8` adds the existing `Blocked.error`
ErrorDetail semantics to unmet requirements, and `CAPABILITY_REVOKE_GRANT = 12`
adds the stable revoke affordance. SpoolOverview.actions MUST include exactly
one targeted RevokeGrant entry per visible grant, carrying a POLICY requirement
and typed cause when protected. Per-grant PutGrant `grant.role` requirements
apply to proposals below ADMINISTRATOR; they do not prohibit role-preserving
edits or upgrades. Clients must consume this advice rather than infer floors.

Rust exports `v2::membership_floor` and TypeScript exports
`@heddleco/api/v2/membership-floor` plus the v2 barrel. Pure helpers evaluate
host-trusted candidates/proposed changes, preserve existing unauthorized
refusals, and compose per-grant action advice. They do not implement host
storage, authentication, visibility, concurrency or scheduling.

Both runtimes execute 30 shared cases in
`tests/fixtures/membership-floors.json`, including personal self/other revoke,
last member/admin, demotion, expiry past/now, agent exclusion, multiple admins,
inherited access, duplicate humans, hidden-member privacy, authorized
non-administrator removals and combined
ancestry/account cascades. Batch helpers evaluate all replacements together;
individual prechecks cannot establish a compound mutation's resulting floor. Independent
`tools/verify-alpha40-membership-guards.py rust` / `ts` probes remove each floor,
require its refusal vectors (including privacy-masked floors) to become allowed
and ordinary assertions to fail,
then restore and require passing results. Operation authorization, privacy and agent-counting guards are
also removed/restored; the denied-operation vector must become allowed with its
authorization guard removed, along with the TS missing-fallback guard. Both probe suites are part of `./tools/verify.sh`.

All seven Rust/npm version and lockfiles advance together to alpha.40.
The orchestrator owns tagging.
